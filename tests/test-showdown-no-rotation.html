🃏 Showdown AI Cards Only Test

Test Purpose

This test verifies that during showdown in the AI Bot Scene, only AI player cards are revealed face-up while human player cards remain face down.

Test Steps

  1. Start the game: The AI Bot Scene should load with 2 AI players and 1 human player
  2. Play through a hand: Take actions (call, fold, raise) to reach showdown
  3. Observe showdown: When showdown occurs, only AI player cards should be revealed
  4. Check AI cards: AI player cards should be face-up with rotation (-12° and +12°)
  5. Check human cards: Human player cards should remain face-down (back of card visible)
